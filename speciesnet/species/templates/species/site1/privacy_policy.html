AquaristSpecies.net Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-10-30

1. Who we are. AquaristSpecies.net is run by {{ SITE_OWNER_DISPLAY_TEXT }}, pending formation or selection of a nonprofit corporation, which will take over this policy when it is formed. Questions or requests: aquaristspecies@gmail.com.

2. Our approach. We collect only what the site needs to connect aquarists and the information they choose to share regarding the species they keep, and to support participation with optional clubs and the CARES conservation and recognition efforts. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not show advertising.

4. Your privacy settings. You decide who can see your personal details. In Edit Profile → Privacy you can set each of these:

SettingOptionsDefault
Show my email addressNever / Signed-in membersSigned-in members
Show my first and last nameNever on public pages / Signed-in membersSigned-in members
Show my state and countryNever / Signed-in membersNever

If you join a club, club members can see your first and last name on club-only pages.

When your email address is hidden, other members can send you an email message through the site without seeing your email address. It is up to you to reply using the sender's email included in the message.

5. What is public anyway. Your display username, and your species pages, photos and written content you contribute (with your photo credit on shared species profile pages).

6. Who else sees your information

  • Clubs you join on the site: the club's members see your name and breeding records for that club's programs.
  • CARES: if you register CARES species or choose a CARES Partner club, the relevant species records are shared with the CARES program, along with your name and email as required for CARES registrations.
  • Service providers that host the site or send email for us (Hostinger.com, Gmail.com), only to run the site.
  • Legal inqiries: if required by law, or to protect users, the public or the site.
  • A successor nonprofit: if the site is transferred to a nonprofit corporation, or on its dissolution to another nonprofit with a similar purpose.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share it for marketing.

7. Keeping and deleting your information. You can change your details and privacy settings at any time. You can delete your account with an "account deletion" request by contacting the site admin or sending an email to aquaristspecies@gmail.com. Once received, your account and all species data associated with the account will be deleted within 30 days, except where we must keep something by law. Species records supporting club programs or the CARES program are removed or made anonymous; totals already published stay.

8. Cookies and analytics. We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in. We count page visits, without advertising features.

9. Security. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and store passwords only in hashed form. No website is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal information, we will tell you as required by law and as soon as practical.

10. Children. You must be at least 16 to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from younger children; if we learn we have, we delete it.

11. Users outside the United States. The site is run from the United States and your information is stored there. You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your information.

12. Changes. If we change this policy in a way that affects how your information is shared, we will post the new version with its date and inform users when it takes effect.

13. Contact. aquaristspecies@gmail.com
