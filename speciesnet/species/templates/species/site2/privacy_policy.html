CaresSpecies.org Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-10-30

1. Who we are. CaresSpecies.org is run by {{ SITE_OWNER_DISPLAY_TEXT }}, pending formation of a nonprofit corporation, which will take over this policy when it is formed. Questions or requests: caresspecies@gmail.com.

2. Our approach. We collect only what the site needs to maintain the CARES Priority List of species and to accept, verify, and maintain records for registered CARES species and recognition programs. Aquarists registering their CARES species can optionally choose a partner club to participate in their supporting local club programs.

4. Who sees your information

  • CARES Registration Approvers, Site Admins.
  • Partner Clubs: if you register CARES species or choose a CARES Partner club, the relevant species records are shared with your CARES club liaison to support the club's local CARES initiatives.
  • Service providers that host the site or send email for us (Hostinger.com, Gmail.com), only to run the site.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect users, the public or the site.
  • A successor nonprofit: if the site is transferred to a nonprofit corporation, or on its dissolution to another nonprofit with a similar purpose.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share it for marketing.

5. Keeping and deleting your information. You can request removal of your CARES registration records at any time. You can request removal of all your CARES records by contacting the site admin or sending an email to caresspecies@gmail.com. Once received and verified, your species records associated with your email account will be deleted or made anonymous within 30 days, except where we must keep something by law.

6. Cookies and analytics. We use only the cookies for Admin and Approver accounts needed for sign-in. We count page visits, without advertising features.

7. Security. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and store passwords only in hashed form. No website is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal information, we will tell you as required by law and as soon as practical.

8. Users outside the United States. The site is run from the United States and your information is stored there. You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your information.

9. Changes. If we change this policy in a way that affects how your information is shared, we will post the new version with its date and inform users when it takes effect.

10. Contact. caresspecies@gmail.com
