'The Rules'

1. Winners agree to make their best effort to care for and breed the fish to help support the CARES Preservation Program by distributing offspring throughout the hobby. All available species have been CARES verified so you can register them immediately.

2. Winners agree to create an AquaristSpecies.net account with the species listed and to register the species with the CARES Preservation Program. By providing your name and email address below you consent to have these steps done automatically for you.

3. If for any reason in the future you are unable to continue caring for these fish, you agree to give them back to a CARES representative so they may be re-homed to continue supporting the CARES program.

4. You may choose up to 3 of the available CARES species. To win *you must be present* at the set time of the raffle. Failure to show forfeits your entry.

5. Only 1 winning species per entry. If you are a species raffle winner you are no longer eligible for species raffled later on the same day.
